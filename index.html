Swaha is a personal recordkeeping app. You enter and manage your own information; it does not connect to bank accounts or move money.

Income & expenses
Record transactions, organize categories and review balances across the accounts you create.
Loans & EMIs
Track payment history and pending amounts, with interest estimates, different first instalments and prepayment planning.
Debts & lent money
Keep track of amounts owed, repayments and money others owe you.
Plans & reminders
Manage bills, commitments, budgets and savings goals, with optional Android notifications.
Your choice of backup

On your phone. In your Drive, if you choose.

Start offline

Use the core tracker without a Google account. Export a local backup file whenever you want an independent copy.

Connect Google

Authorize access to Swaha’s own app-data folder and enable daily backup. Each account stores its own backup in its own Google Drive.

Restore with a preview

Connect the same Google account to fetch the latest verified backup. Review and confirm before replacing the records on your phone.

One current cloud backup. A new upload is verified before older Swaha copies are removed. A failed upload keeps the previous verified copy. Daily background work needs internet and may be delayed by Android; backup is not live multi-device synchronization.

During early access, Google connection may be limited to invited test accounts while public authorization setup is completed. Local recordkeeping and local backups do not require Google sign-in.
