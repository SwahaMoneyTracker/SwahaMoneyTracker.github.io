Swaha Money Tracker

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 25 November 2026

Your finance records are stored on your device. Google Drive backup is optional and sends a copy to the Google account you select. This policy describes the current Android app and this public information website.

Information you enter

Swaha stores the information you choose to record, including account names and opening balances, income and expense transactions, categories, loans and EMI schedules, debts, lent money, bills, commitments, budgets, savings goals, investment records, notes, receipt attachments and preferences. Core recordkeeping does not require a Google account. Swaha does not request your bank-login password or initiate bank payments.

Storage on your device

The ledger is kept in the app’s private storage. The app also prepares a local snapshot for background backup. When you restore a backup, a recovery copy of the previous ledger is saved in private app storage; that copy can remain until replaced by a later restore or until app storage is cleared. Manually exported backup and CSV files are saved or shared only to destinations you select.

Google account and Drive access

If you connect Google, Swaha uses the account email you select to identify the backup destination. Google handles account selection and permission approval. Swaha does not receive your Google password. Access tokens are used for the authorized operation; Swaha does not store long-lived refresh tokens in its ledger or backup files.

The automatic backup feature requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, which permits access to Swaha’s own application-data folder. This feature does not request permission to read Gmail messages or browse unrelated Drive documents. A manual file-picker import or export separately accesses the particular file or location you choose.

How Google data is used

Google access is used to find, upload, verify, replace and restore Swaha backup files. A backup includes your recorded ledger, settings and any attached receipts. It is stored in the selected account’s private Swaha app-data folder. Each Google account has its own backup. The app does not send your ledger or Google access tokens to a developer-operated server.

Swaha’s use of Google user data is limited to the backup and restore features described here. The app does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, personal profiling, unrelated analytics or AI-model training. Google provides the account and storage service under its Privacy Policy.

Backup schedule and retention

Enabling daily backup schedules Android background work that requires internet and a sufficient battery level. Android restrictions, force-stop, unavailable storage or removed permissions can delay or prevent an upload. Check the last successful upload shown in the app.

Normally one current verified cloud backup is retained. A replacement is uploaded and verified before older Swaha copies are removed. Interrupted uploads preserve the prior verified copy; failed cleanup can temporarily leave extra copies, and the app reports cleanup as pending. The next successful backup retries cleanup. Manually exported files are not automatically removed by this process. Backups are copies, not live multi-device synchronization.

PIN, fingerprint and permissions

If enabled, the app PIN’s verification record and retry counters are protected using Android Keystore-backed encryption. Fingerprint matching is performed by Android; Swaha receives an authentication result, not your fingerprint template. Your app PIN, PIN verifier, fingerprint data and device security keys are not included in financial backups.

JSON backup files contain readable financial records, so protect access to them. An app lock does not encrypt a JSON export. Google requests use HTTPS, and Google controls storage-service security. The current app does not provide a separate user-controlled, end-to-end encryption password for Drive backups.

Notification permission is used for reminders when you enable them. The system file picker provides access to selected receipt or backup files. Google connection and internet access support the optional Drive feature. You may deny optional permissions and continue using the available offline features.

Your choices and deletion

  • Edit or delete records in Swaha. Linked records may need to be removed or reassigned first to preserve ledger consistency.
  • Turn off daily backup or disconnect Google in the app to stop future uploads. Disconnecting does not delete existing cloud copies or your phone’s ledger.
  • Remove Swaha’s hidden app data through Google Drive settings under Manage apps. See Google’s storage-management guidance. You may also revoke Swaha’s access in your Google Account’s third-party connections.
  • Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling normally removes the app’s local records, snapshots and security settings. Files you exported or shared, and cloud copies, remain until you delete them from their respective destinations.
  • After deleting records, a later successful backup replaces the prior cloud copy. Keep independent exports only as long as you need them. Restore replaces phone data after confirmation and leaves daily backup paused until you enable it again.

Sharing, support and this website

The reviewed app build has no developer analytics or advertising service. If you use an export/share action, the receiving application or person gets the information you choose to send and their policies apply. If you voluntarily post a support issue, GitHub and the publisher can see what you post; do not include financial records, passwords, PINs or backup files.

This website is a static information site hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no finance-entry forms, added analytics scripts or advertising trackers. GitHub logs website visitors’ IP addresses for security and handles hosting requests under its Privacy Statement.

Contact and changes

For general technical or privacy questions, use the Swaha support page. Public issues must not include sensitive information. For a private message, use the user-support email shown on Swaha’s Google permission screen. We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change; the date above identifies the current published policy.
