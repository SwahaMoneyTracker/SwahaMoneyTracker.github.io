Swaha is a personal finance recordkeeping and planning tool. You enter your own records and control the accounts and information you add. The app is not a bank, lender, broker or payment processor. Estimates and reminders

Loan schedules, interest, prepayment calculations, balances and reports depend on the information entered and the calculation methods supported by the app. They may differ from a lender’s actual statement, fees, daily-interest rules or settlement terms. Check important amounts and due dates against your original records and lender statements. Swaha does not provide individualized investment, tax or legal advice.

Android permissions, battery restrictions, time-zone settings, device state and operating-system scheduling can affect reminders and daily backups. Do not rely on a notification as your only record of a payment deadline.

Records, security and backups

You retain control of the records you enter. Use only information and Google accounts you are authorized to use. Protect your device, PIN, Google account and exported files. Keep an independent local backup when changing phones or installing a build that requires uninstalling a previous version.

Google backup is optional and requires your authorization, available storage and a working connection. It normally retains one current verified copy, replacing older Swaha copies after a successful upload. It is not a complete version-history service or live multi-device sync. Check upload status before relying on a cloud copy. Restoring replaces the current device ledger after confirmation; review the preview first.
